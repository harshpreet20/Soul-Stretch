## Image specifications (recommended)

- Dimensions: 800x800px minimum (square crop works best for cards)
- Background: White or transparent PNG preferred for clean display on dark (#1b1b1b) card backgrounds
- File size: Under 500KB per image (use tinypng.com to compress)
